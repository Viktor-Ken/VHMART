Contact a vendor

VHMART connects you with vendors directly. VHMART does not process purchases, so every question goes to the vendor who sells the product.

  1. Find a product in the marketplace and open it.
  2. Choose I’m interested to go to the vendor’s profile.
  3. Pick Contact vendor to reach them on their phone, WhatsApp or social pages (you will be asked to accept the Terms of Use first), or Make an enquiry to send a message they can read and answer.
