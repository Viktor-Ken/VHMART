Optimise product photos

Older product photos are large, which slows the site down for customers. This page finds them and shrinks them to the same size new uploads get (800 px wide at most, WebP, about 70 KB). Nothing changes until you press the shrink button, and you must download a backup first.

Press “Scan photos” to start. Scanning only reads; it changes nothing.
